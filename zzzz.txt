All services are containers managed by Compose. Being in the Docker network lets containers communicate by service name, but only services with ports: are reachable from the host/browser. For example, Adminer is public on port 8081, but it reaches MariaDB privately using mariadb:3306. Static site and dashboard do not strictly need internal networking, but they are attached to the project network for consistency. FTP mainly shares the WordPress volume, and its published ports allow external FTP clients to connect.
